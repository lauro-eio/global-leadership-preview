More Than One Way Out: What the Evidence Says About Option Generation Under Pressure

More Than One Way Out: What the Evidence Says About Option Generation Under Pressure

When time is short, supervisors often invent one counter and call it negotiation. Research on integrative bargaining shows that generating multiple packages before choosing improves joint outcomes (Pruitt, 1981; Thompson, 1991).

Fixed-pie bias

Negotiators systematically assume a fixed pie and miss trades across issues (Thompson & Hastie, 1990). On a floor, that looks like trading only on hours when sequence, skill mix, and temporary help are also available.

Brainstorm separately from decide

Separating invention from evaluation increases the number of options on the table (Osborn's brainstorming logic applied in negotiation settings; see Pruitt, 1981). A two-minute "no judging" list beats an immediate yes/no on the first idea.

Logrolling

Trading across differently valued issues — logrolling — creates mutual gain without splitting every issue down the middle (Froman & Cohen, 1970; Pruitt, 1981). A peer who values weekend off more than Friday OT can trade if you value Friday coverage more.

Floor rule

Minimum two packages that each side could live with before you propose. If you only have one, you are still in positional mode.

Selected references

  • Froman, L. A., & Cohen, M. D. (1970). Compromise and logroll. Behavioral Science, 15, 1–15.
  • Pruitt, D. G. (1981). Negotiation Behavior. Academic Press.
  • Thompson, L. (1991). Information exchange in negotiation. Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 27(2), 161–179.
  • Thompson, L., & Hastie, R. (1990). Social perception in negotiation. Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes, 47(1), 98–123.

The False Choice Between Saturday OT and Missing the Truck

The False Choice Between Saturday OT and Missing the Truck

I told my team it was Saturday OT or a missed shipment. That was lazy.

We actually had three levers: pull two certified temps from sister plant, move a non-critical SKU, and run a short OT block Friday. I had framed a false binary because I was tired. Once we listed options without judging, the truck left without a full Saturday call-in.

Now I force myself to write two packages before I open my mouth.

Framework — Option Ladder for Floor Deals

Framework — Option Ladder for Floor Deals

Rung 1 — Issues

List every issue in play (time, headcount, sequence, quality hold, cost owner).

Rung 2 — Invent (2 minutes)

No evaluating. Aim for 4+ raw ideas.

Rung 3 — Package

Build Package A and Package B. Each must give both sides something they care about.

Rung 4 — Propose

Offer both packages. Ask which fits better — don't defend only one.

Take-away · Key notes

Key Notes — Two Options Minimum Before You Propose

Key Notes — Two Options Minimum Before You Propose

Unit goal: Invent multiple workable options under pressure instead of a single counter-position.

Core ideas

  • Fixed-pie bias hides trades (Thompson & Hastie, 1990).
  • Invent first, evaluate second.
  • Logroll across issues with different value weights.
  • Two packages minimum.

Checklist

  1. List issues (not just the hot number).
  2. Brainstorm 3+ options, no judging for two minutes.
  3. Build two packages both sides could accept.
  4. Propose packages, not single concessions.
